HOW TO IMPORT
1. Start the backend once so it creates backend/saferoute.db.
2. Close the backend window.
3. Double-click IMPORT_SAMPLE_DATA.bat.
4. Restart START_BACKEND.bat and refresh the website.
5. Check the map and popup; treat these points as demonstration/sample records only.

HOW TO PRESENT THIS IN A COMPETITION
- Explain the problem: route planning normally focuses on distance/time, not nearby
  historical road-risk indicators and emergency support.
- Demonstrate two routes and the sample-data proximity indicator.
- Show nearby hospitals, pharmacies, police, fire, fuel and repair services.
- Show the emergency calling shortcuts and clear limitations.
- Explain the verification pipeline needed before public use: official source,
  source URL/document, date of retrieval, coordinate accuracy, duplicate checks,
  human review, and a visible verified/unverified badge.
- Do not claim that this sample is official, that it predicts crashes, or that it
  detects every live accident. The current live endpoint only displays incidents
  entered by an authorised operator.

SAFETY & LIMITATIONS
- Route alternatives come from a public routing service and do not include live
  traffic or guaranteed road-safety conditions.
- The point proximity score is a prototype indicator, not a validated risk model.
- OpenStreetMap service listings may be incomplete or outdated.
- Emergency numbers and availability can vary; 112 is India's pan-India emergency
  number. In a real emergency, follow official emergency-service instructions.
- Do not let this prototype distract drivers. Use it before starting a journey or
  ask a passenger to operate it. Never interact with a phone while driving.
- Do not publish unverified personal reports or identifying details.
