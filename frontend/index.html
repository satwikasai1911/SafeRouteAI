SafeRoute AI

Accident hotspots, safer routes and emergency help · Team Abhinava, SCSVMV

Citizen road-safety decision support — prototype. This build can display the supplied Tamil Nadu sample dataset, compare route proximity to recorded points, and locate nearby help. The supplied CSV is unverified sample data, not an official accident register. Route time is an estimate and does not include live traffic, weather, road closures or police advisories. Always follow road signs and official instructions.

What to do in an emergency

  1. Stop in a safe place, switch on hazard lights, place a reflective triangle behind the vehicle.
  2. Call 112 or 108. Give your exact location (landmark, road name, nearest town).
  3. Do not move an injured person unless there is fire or danger. Press firmly on heavy bleeding.
  4. Keep with you: first aid kit, torch, power bank, spare tyre and jack, water, vehicle papers and insurance details.
  5. Note the vehicle numbers, take photos, and inform the police and your insurer.
